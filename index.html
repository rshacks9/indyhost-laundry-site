FAQ

The questions hosts ask first.

How fast is the turnaround?

Same day. We pick up around 11am on turn day and have clean linens back at the property by about 4pm, ahead of the next check-in.

Do I have to be there for pickup or delivery?

No. We coordinate access with you or your cleaner once, then it runs on its own. You do nothing on turn day.

What if a sheet or towel gets stained or damaged?

On the Hosted Linen Program we own the linens, so normal wear is on us, not you. We track inventory per property and replace worn sets as part of the service. Every turn is photo-documented so there is never a dispute about condition.

How is this different from just using my cleaner?

Your cleaner is great at cleaning and rarely great at laundry. We are a dedicated linen service: consistent washing, hotel-standard folding, same-day timing, and we never run out of clean sets. We do not solicit your cleaner's other work. We make their turn day easier.

What does it cost?

Most hosts run the Hosted Linen Program at a $250 one-time setup per property, then $45 to $65 per turn with a $200 monthly minimum. Pickup-and-Return starts at $25 to $35 per turn with a $100 minimum. We will send exact numbers for your properties.

Do you serve gyms and salons too?

Yes. Our Boutique Fitness Towel Service handles weekly towel turnover for gyms, studios, and salons in Broad Ripple, Carmel, and Fishers, billed by the piece with a $200 weekly minimum.
